The meeting is over. Your notes are full. A week later, someone asks: “Why did we decide that?” You can find the discussion, the suggestions and the action items. Following the reasoning takes a little more digging.

I’m building notably. to keep that thread within reach. It gives business analysts a place to turn notes into questions, gather answers and record a conclusion with its reasoning attached.

## Give the interesting bit somewhere to go

Imagine your team wants to shorten an application form because “people give up halfway through”. Is length really the problem?

In notably., you can select that line in your notes and turn it into a linked question. Add a task to investigate the awkward steps. Perhaps an interview answer points to confusing eligibility rules. Now you have something to examine before deciding which fields to remove.

An answered question or a ticked-off task doesn’t settle the assumption. You choose which answers matter, explain your reasoning and record a conclusion.

## Keep the why

- **The source stays close.** Linked questions and tasks lead back to the passage that started them.
- **The reasoning stays readable.** Conclusions retain snapshots of the evidence considered, and earlier conclusions remain in history.
- **The work stays connected.** Notes, questions and tasks belong to one local workspace, with the same records available across its views.

I’m developing notably. as an offline Mac app, and it’s still a personal project in progress.
